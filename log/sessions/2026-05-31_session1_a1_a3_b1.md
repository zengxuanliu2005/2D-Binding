# Session 1 — A1 + A3 + B1

## What we did

### A1 (rigid K2D 22% gap)
Tested whether the gap is numerical (h-grid + bond-sample undersampling per
stage_essay §4.5 diagnosis). Re-ran rigid raw partition at Δh = 0.02 σ and
200 bond-samples (10× / 8× tighter). Point estimate moved from 9858.9 → 9843.7
nm² (−0.15 %). **The §4.5 diagnosis is wrong**: the gap is NOT a numerical
artifact.

Wrote diagnostic `scripts/diagnose_bound_vs_unbound.py` (renamed from
`diagnose_rigid_sample_bias.py` in session 3). Compared bound vs unbound R
chain geometry across systems:

| system | bound ⟨z⟩ (nm) | unbound ⟨z⟩ (nm) | Δz | bound tilt | unbound tilt | Δtilt |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| rigid | 9.162 | 8.902 | **−0.26** | 17.9° | 24.9° | **+7°** |
| semi  | 7.945 | 7.258 | −0.69    | 41.1° | 52.5° | +11° |
| flex  | 6.629 | 5.146 | −1.48    | 44.0° | 58.2° | +14° |

Bound chains are systematically more upright than unbound. Most binding-
competent geometries get absorbed into the bound pool, leaving "geometry-
unfavorable" configs in the unbound pool. With rigid σ_K2D(l) = 0.62 nm
narrow, even Δz = −0.26 nm propagates to ~25 % K2D underestimate (matches
observed 22 % gap).

This **unifies §4.5 (rigid 22% gap) and §5.4 (chain-response shape
mismatch)** as one physical issue: equilibrium-sampled unbound geometry ≠
"would-be-bound" geometry.

### A3 (S1-S23 four-term bootstrap)
Refactored `phd_inputs.py` to expose `system_inputs_from_arrays` so
`phd_closure.py` can resample frames per system without re-parsing npz.
Added `--bootstrap`, `--n-bootstrap`, `--n-jobs` flags. Ran n=200 bootstrap.

σ_sum ≈ 0.03 kBT for all three pairs. The gaps from target are:
- flex−rigid: +0.43 kBT = **14σ** (systematic, not noise)
- semi−rigid: +0.03 kBT = 1σ (consistent)
- semi−flex:  −0.38 kBT = **13σ**

Confirms the known end-volume × rotation double-counting in S1-S23.

### B1 (s25 reimpl as 5th method)
New `scripts/phd_closure_s25.py` (later renamed `closure_wlc_three_term.py`).
Implements trans + rot + Marko-Siggia integrated WLC with principled
L_c = 12 nm (12 protein bonds × 1.0 σ per CLAUDE.md).

Result: ΔΔF = 5.23 / 2.05 / −3.18 kBT vs PPT s25's 3.64 / 2.47 / −1.18.
1.5-2 kBT gap means PPT s25 is **not parameter-free**. Senior implicitly
uses a system-specific L_c. → ADR 001 documents our L_c = 12 nm choice
pending senior clarification.

Added as method 5 in `reconcile_methods.py`.
