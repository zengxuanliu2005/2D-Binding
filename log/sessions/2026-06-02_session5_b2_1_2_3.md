# Session 5 — B2.1 + B2.2 + B2.3

## What we did

Three derivation steps in one session, each commit + derivation/ folder
with 5 standard md files (00 intent / 01 setup / 03 result / 04 numerics /
05 open questions).

### B2.1 — P(R; lp, Lc) via discrete WLC MC (commit 6cb511f)

`scripts/k2d_l_wlc_theory.py::WLCChain.sample_endpoints`:
- N = 12 bonds (Lc = 12 nm, b = 1 nm)
- κ inverted from lp via Langevin function
- 200 K chains × 3 systems × 8 workers → 1.3 s wall

Acceptance: √⟨R²⟩(MC) matches continuum WLC ⟨R²⟩ formula:

| system | lp (nm) | √⟨R²⟩_MC | √⟨R²⟩_continuum | ratio |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| rigid | 84.60 | 11.725 | 11.723 | 1.000 |
| semi  |  8.18 |  9.681 |  9.662 | 1.002 |
| flex  |  1.14 |  5.161 |  4.976 | 1.037 |

All within 4 % of continuum. ✓

Senior PPT slide-16 Re comparison shows 17-21 % under-prediction for
rigid/semi — open Q1 (ecto vs full chain Lc). → ADR 001.

### B2.2 — P_z(z; lp, Lc, k_a) with anchor cone (commit 8b6b540)

`apply_anchor_cone(R_chain, κ_a)`:
- Small-angle Rayleigh sampling of θ_a from κ_a = 255/1.1 = 231.8 rad⁻²
- Rotates chain endpoints into lab frame

Production stats:

| system | ⟨z_c⟩ | ⟨z⟩_MC | ⟨z_c⟩·⟨cos θ_a⟩ | σ_z |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| rigid | 11.26 | 11.21 | 11.21 | **0.625** |
| semi  |  6.69 |  6.66 |  6.66 | **3.18** |
| flex  |  1.72 |  1.71 |  1.71 | **2.79** |

`apply_anchor_cone` matches the independent-rotation analytic prediction
to 4 decimal places — implementation verified.

**Key footgun documented**: σ_z scales as **1/κ_a**, not 1/√κ_a. The
first draft of `rigid_limit_z_stats` had it wrong; pilot Sanity A caught
it with a 50× discrepancy. Reason: cos θ is quadratic in θ for small θ,
so Var(cos θ) = ¼ Var(θ²) = 1/κ_a². See `derivation/02/04_numerics.md`
docstring of `rigid_limit_z_stats`.

### B2.3 — K2D(l) convolution + σ_K2D (commit bf20668)

`k2d_l_curve(z_R, z_L, l_grid)`:
- Hard-gate kernel: v(dz) = π(rcut² − dz²) for |dz| < rcut, rcut = 1.5 nm
- z < 0 truncation ON (drop chains that fold through the membrane)
- l-grid: 260 points × 0.1 nm spacing

Production:

| pair | K2D,max (nm²) | l* (nm) | σ_K2D (nm) | measured ξ_RL | ratio |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| rigid×rigid | 4.85 | 22.9 | **1.42** | 0.685 | 2.07 |
| semi×semi  | 1.11 | 18.7 | **5.45** | 2.076 | 2.63 |
| flex×flex  | 1.32 |  3.5 | **3.91** | 2.253 | 1.73 |

σ_K2D systematically over-predicts measured ξ_RL by 1.7-2.6×. Three
contributing factors documented in `derivation/03/03_result.md §3.3`:
(a) WLC σ_z is 1.8× wider than measured σ(R_z); (b) hard-gate kernel
adds rcut/√5 ≈ 0.67 nm in quadrature; (c) ξ_RL ≠ σ_K2D literally —
Weikl 2016 convolution adds σ_p.

**σ_K2D ordering reversal** (semi > flex, violating expected monotone):
flex P_z is more symmetric and loses 40 % of mass to z<0 truncation,
compressing σ_K2D. Semi only loses ~5 %. Documented in
`derivation/03/05_open_questions.md` Q2. → ADR 004.

## Open questions (carry to B2.4 + B2.5)

1. σ_K2D 2× over-prediction. → B2.4 will use the **ratio test**
   (σ_K2D(rigid)/σ_K2D(flex)) instead of absolute σ_K2D, side-stepping
   the calibration gap.
2. F_conf comparison: B2.5 will compute F_conf = -ln P_z(D_bound) from

   triangulation of the L_c sensitivity.
